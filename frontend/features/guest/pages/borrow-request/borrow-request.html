How to Pose for Your Book Capture

Follow these steps
  1. Face the camera
    Look directly at it.
  2. Hold the book
    Front cover faces the camera.
  3. Position the book
    Below your chest, inside the guide.
  4. Use good lighting
    Face and cover must be clear.
  5. Remove obstructions
    No hats, masks, or covered face.
